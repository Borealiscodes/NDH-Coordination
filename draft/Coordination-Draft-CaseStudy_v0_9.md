# **Coordination-Draft-CaseStudy_v0_9.md**  
### *A minimal routing case study for NDH‑Coordination*

## **1. Scenario Overview**

An exploratory artifact is produced inside **NDH‑Research‑Pilot**.  
It contains three mixed components:

- a semantic framing  
- an early algebraic structure  
- a hint of simulation intent  

In Research‑Pilot, these components often remain entangled.  
In NDH‑Coordination, they must be separated and routed cleanly.

This case study demonstrates how Coordination performs that routing.

---

## **2. Artifact Breakdown**

The artifact contains:

### **2.1 Semantic Framing**  
A conceptual description or narrative structure.

### **2.2 Algebraic Structure**  
Early mathematical form — not yet formalized, but recognizable.

### **2.3 Simulation Intent**  
A suggestion that the artifact might later be used in a runtime or modeling context.

### **2.4 Provenance Context**  
Metadata describing where the artifact came from and why it exists.

### **2.5 Exploratory Origin**  
The fact that the artifact was created in Research‑Pilot.

---

## **3. Correct Routing (NDH‑Coordination)**

NDH‑Coordination decomposes the artifact and routes each component to its proper subsystem:

- **Semantic framing → Constellation**  
- **Algebraic structure → Triadic‑Core**  
- **Simulation intent → Simulation‑Suite**  
- **Provenance context → Provenance**  
- **Exploratory origin → Research‑Pilot**  

This routing is altitude‑aware but non‑activating.  
No subsystem is triggered; each simply receives the part that belongs to it.

---

## **4. Outcome**

The artifact is decomposed into its natural components.  
Each subsystem receives only what it is designed to handle.  
No altitude collapse occurs.  
No subsystem absorbs responsibilities that belong elsewhere.

NDH remains coherent.

This is the core purpose of NDH‑Coordination.

---

## **5. Why This Case Study Matters**

The original Research‑Pilot draft showed how artifacts can drift:

- algebra leaking into semantics  
- simulation leaking into architecture  
- provenance being ignored  
- exploratory artifacts becoming structural artifacts  

This case study demonstrates the correction:  
**Coordination restores clarity by routing each part to its proper home.**
