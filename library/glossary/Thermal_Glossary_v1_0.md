# 🜁 **NDH‑Glossary Additions — Thermal Vocabulary (v1.0)**  
### *Glossary Layer • A0–A2*  
### *Conceptual Definitions Only*

---

## **Active‑Warm Layer**  
**Definition:**  
A conceptual layer in active circulation. Altitude‑bounded, membrane‑protected, drift‑neutral, and lineage‑anchored. Contains artifacts currently in use within NDH‑Coordination.

**Notes:**  
Non‑diagnostic. Non‑routing. Non‑holonomy.

---

## **Archival‑Cold Layer**  
**Definition:**  
A sealed, inert, non‑interactive conceptual layer used for archival storage of retired or superseded artifacts. No altitude transitions occur within this layer.

**Notes:**  
Corresponds to NDH‑PROVENANCE.

---

## **Warm‑Stable**  
**Definition:**  
An active‑warm artifact whose lineage is anchored. Stability derives from altitude anchoring rather than invariant pinning.

**Notes:**  
Lineage‑dependent.

---

## **Warm‑Volatile**  
**Definition:**  
An active‑warm artifact governed by invariants. Volatility derives from pinning rather than anchoring.

**Notes:**  
Invariant‑dependent.

---

## **Warm‑to‑Cold Routing**  
**Definition:**  
The process of retiring an active‑warm artifact into archival‑cold storage. Involves lineage freezing, version sealing, and removal from active governance.

**Notes:**  
Library → NDH‑PROVENANCE.

---

## **Cold‑to‑Warm Retrieval**  
**Definition:**  
The process of reintroducing an archival‑cold artifact into active‑warm circulation. Requires re‑anchoring, re‑indexing, and re‑membraning.

**Notes:**  
NDH‑PROVENANCE → Library (rare).
