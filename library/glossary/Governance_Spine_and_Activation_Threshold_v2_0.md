## ⭐ **1 — Governance Spine (Glossary Entry v2.0)**  
**Governance Spine**  
*Definition:*  
The **Governance Spine** is the dormant, altitude‑sovereign structural backbone that governs how NDH artifacts relate across semantic, lineage, and membrane boundaries. It defines the invariant conditions under which governance logic may be evaluated, without executing or activating any sealed‑layer behavior. The spine stabilizes altitude discipline and prevents drift, inversion, recursion, or membrane contamination.

*Properties:*  
- dormant  
- sealed  
- lineage‑locked  
- membrane‑sovereign  
- altitude‑disciplined  
- non‑activating  
- structural‑only  
- drift‑neutral  

*Function:*  
Provides the invariant framework for sequencing, variation, promotion, integration, and constitutional evaluation across NDH governance altitudes.

## ⭐ **2 — Activation Threshold (Glossary Entry v2.0)**  
**Activation Threshold**  
*Definition:*  
An **Activation Threshold** is a governance‑grade boundary determining whether an NDH artifact may evolve, upgrade, or transition altitudes. Thresholds are **non‑trigger** and **non‑event‑based**: they rely solely on structural, semantic, ethical, scientific, or sovereignty conditions being satisfied. They prevent premature activation, altitude drift, membrane inversion, lineage fracture, or governance recursion.

*Properties:*  
- non‑trigger  
- non‑event‑based  
- conceptual  
- dormant  
- sealed  
- altitude‑neutral  
- structural‑only  
- membrane‑safe  

*Function:*  
Ensures evolution only occurs when all required governance conditions are met, preserving altitude discipline and preventing sealed‑layer contamination.
