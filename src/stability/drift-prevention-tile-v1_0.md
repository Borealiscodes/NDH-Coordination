# ⭐ 1 — Purpose

The Drift‑Prevention Tile defines the **rules, membranes, and constraints** that prevent:

- altitude drift  
- membrane drift  
- routing drift  
- posture drift  
- translation drift  
- substrate drift  

It is the **second tile** in the A10 Stability Ridge.

---

# ⭐ 2 — Drift‑Prevention Rules (DP‑Series)

### **DP‑1 — Altitude Drift Prohibition**  
A12, A11, and A10 must remain altitude‑distinct.

### **DP‑2 — Membrane Drift Prohibition**  
Posture, holonomy, and routing membranes must not blur or merge.

### **DP‑3 — Routing Drift Prohibition**  
Routing logic must remain passive, reversible, and substrate‑sealed.

### **DP‑4 — Translation Drift Prohibition**  
Zen‑Bridge translation seams must remain stable and non‑absorptive.

### **DP‑5 — Substrate Drift Prohibition**  
MCP, MSO, and MCB substrate geometry must remain sealed and drift‑neutral.

### **DP‑6 — Adjacency Drift Prohibition**  
Constellation adjacency must not leak into developer altitudes.
