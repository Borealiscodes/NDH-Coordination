### **SSR‑1 — Seam Sovereignty**  
The A11↔A10 seam must remain sovereign and non‑absorptive.

### **SSR‑2 — Translation Safety**  
Holonomy translation must not leak into coordination routing.

### **SSR‑3 — Posture‑Aware Traversal**  
All seam traversal must respect posture constraints inherited from A12.

### **SSR‑4 — Drift‑Neutral Movement**  
Routing across the seam must remain drift‑neutral and reversible.

### **SSR‑5 — Membrane Integrity**  
The seam membrane must remain sealed against CAUSA, runtime primitives, and developer altitudes.

### **SSR‑6 — Substrate Anchoring**  
Seam traversal must bind to MCP/MSO/MCB substrate geometry.
