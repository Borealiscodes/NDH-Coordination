- no upward leakage  
- no downward collapse  
- reversible altitude transitions  
- morphism closure under functor application  

This keeps the algebraic altitude tier stable.

Meta‑functors are powerful and potentially recursive.

- drift‑neutrality  
- bounded recursion  
- reversible meta‑operations  
- non‑absorptive meta‑functor behavior  

This prevents meta‑functor runaway conditions.

### 🜃 **Functor Categories ↔ Altitude Separation (RS‑3)**

- translation‑safe functor chaining  
- no seam inversion
