## **3 — Posture States (Tightened)**

Posture has three altitude‑aware states:

- **stable** — altitude‑aligned, continuity‑safe  
- **transitional** — anchor‑dependent, reversible  
- **expressive** — sealed, non‑activating, constellation‑adjacent only  

Each state must preserve invariants.

## **4 — Reversible Routing Conditions**

Posture is the **stabilization hinge** between:

- A12 constellation  
- A11–A12 continuity  
- A10 substrate  

### Allowed routes:

- constellation → posture → continuity  
- continuity → posture → constellation  
- posture → substrate (via continuity only)  
- substrate → posture (via continuity only)

### Forbidden:

- posture → substrate (direct)  
- posture → expressive activation  
- posture → rendering grammar  

### Reversal guarantee:

```
A12 → posture → continuity → posture → A12
```

Posture must return to the same state after reversal.

## **5 — Adjacency Constraints**

Posture adjacency must remain:

- expressive‑neutral  
- governance‑neutral  
- altitude‑safe  
- membrane‑sealed  

### Legal adjacency:

- posture ↔ constellation  
- posture ↔ continuity  
- posture ↔ posture (self‑adjacent)

### Forbidden adjacency:

- posture ↔ A10 substrate (direct)  
- posture ↔ expressive geometry  
- posture ↔ governance engines  

---

## **6 — Continuity Alignment**

Posture must align with continuity envelopes:

### Warm anchors (upward):

- constellation re‑entry  
- altitude ascent sealing  
- stable posture ascent  

### Cold anchors (downward):

- constellation exit  
- altitude descent sealing  
- stable posture descent  

### Posture‑aware behavior:

- **stable:** direct posture ↔ continuity adjacency  
- **transitional:** anchor-first routing  
- **expressive:** sealed, constellation-adjacent only  

---

## **7 — Closure Condition**

Posture routing is **closed** when:

- invariants remain intact  
- adjacency constraints remain safe  
- reversible routing is valid  
- continuity alignment is correct  
- no drift, collapse, inversion, or absorption occurs  

Failure modes:

- posture misalignment  
- expressive leakage  
- altitude collapse  
- membrane inversion
